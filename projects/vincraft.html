PROJECT SCOPE

From discovering posts
to running a community.

VinCraft organizes community posts into categories such as Builds, Redstone, News, Tutorial, and Mods. Visitors can browse posts and narrow the collection by title or category.

The application distinguishes guests, registered crafters, and administrators. Crafters can publish and edit posts, attach images and video links, and interact through likes, comments, and reports. Administrators have dedicated pages for managing users, categories, posts, and reports.

IMPLEMENTATION

Connecting the interface
to stored data.

PHP handles sessions, form processing, and role checks. MySQLi connects the application to a relational database with seven tables: users, categories, posts, comments, likes, resource files, and reports.

The schema links interactions to their users and posts through foreign keys, with unique user–post pairs for likes and reports. JavaScript supports the report dialogs, while shared navigation, footer, and CSS keep the pages consistent.
