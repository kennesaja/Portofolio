PROJECT SCOPE

A learning path
with room to practice.

Knowl Online is designed for Indonesian elementary school students. Its grade selector covers Kelas 1–6, with subject pages that connect descriptions, video links, module links, and quizzes. The project's stated educational focus is SDG 4: Quality Education.

The PHP application includes registration and login, multiple-choice quizzes, score calculation, explanations for incorrect answers, and a history of quiz attempts. Administrators have separate pages for adding lessons and questions.

Group coursework. The previews below follow the Grade 1 mathematics content supplied with the project; they do not represent a complete curriculum or a deployed learning service.

APPLICATION FEATURES

Learn, practice, and review.

Features identified in the submitted source code.

01 / DISCOVERY

Choose a grade and subject

A grade selector for Kelas 1–6 leads to subject cards populated from the course database.

02 / LESSONS

Bring learning resources together

Lesson pages combine a description, a video link, a module link, and a route into the associated quiz.

03 / PRACTICE

Review answers and attempts

The result page calculates a score on a 100-point scale, identifies incorrect answers, and shows their explanations. Quiz attempts are saved for the student's history page.

04 / ADMINISTRATION

Add lessons and quiz content

An administrator dashboard shows user and subject counts. Forms let administrators add lessons and questions with four answer options, a correct answer, and an explanation.

IMPLEMENTATION

Linking lessons
to learning history.

PHP handles account sessions, course selection, answer processing, and administrator forms. MySQLi connects the application to the database; the supplied export identifies MariaDB as the database server.

Four tables organize the application: users, courses, questions, and quiz history. Foreign keys connect questions to courses and recorded quiz attempts to a student and a course. Shared HTML templates and CSS provide the interface.
