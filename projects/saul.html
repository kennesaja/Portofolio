THE DESIGN QUESTION

What if a purchase
started with a pause?

Limited-time offers and social pressure can make a purchase feel urgent. SAUL explores ways to bring daily spending, saving goals, and reflection into that decision, with proposed guardrails both inside the app and before online checkout.

I worked as part of Team ShowTime with Adrian Aulia Pulungan, Christian Andrew, and Muhammad Faiz Zaldi. Our project earned second place in the UI/UX competition at Information System Festival 2026.

Competition prototype. The screens show proposed interactions and sample financial data.
